03 / DOWNSTREAM TRANSFER

Same adapters.
New worlds to control.

Move the few-step and CFG LoRAs into a downstream model in the same family. Keep sampling fast; adjust guidance independently.

No downstream retraining. These adapters are reused from the base model. All three results are shown together: only the distilled CFG LoRA weight changes, while the few-step weight stays fixed at 1.
